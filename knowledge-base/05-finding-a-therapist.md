# 5. Finding the right speech pathologist

Australia first, then global telehealth. Verified against each provider's own site in September 2026 — **confirm fees, waitlists and availability directly**, they change.

---

## The single most useful fact about choosing

From the Stamurai team's own marketing, and it is true: most general speech pathologists see very few stuttering cases per year. Fluency is a sub-specialty. **A generalist paediatric speech pathologist is not automatically a good choice for stuttering.**

Ask every clinic these five questions before booking:

1. **How many children who stutter do you currently have on your caseload?** (A number under five a year is a red flag.)
2. **Which stuttering programs are you trained in, and by whom?** (You want a specific answer: Lidcombe, Oakville, Camperdown, Palin, Van Riper modification — not "we use a range of approaches".)
3. **How do you address the emotional and social side, not just fluency?** (If there is no answer, keep looking. See §1 and §4.)
4. **What measures will you use, and will I be keeping daily severity ratings?**
5. **What is your waitlist, and do you offer telehealth?**

## Australia — national and specialist

### Australian Stuttering Treatment Centre (ASRC clinic), UTS Sydney
**The strongest single recommendation in Australia.** The clinical arm of the Australian Stuttering Research Centre — the group that *developed* the Lidcombe, Camperdown, Westmead and Oakville programs.

- Level 13, Building One, 15 Broadway, Ultimo NSW 2007 (near Central Station)
- In person: Wednesdays. **Telehealth: Monday to Friday via Zoom, all ages, Australia-wide and internationally.**
- Programs: Lidcombe, School Age, Syllable Timed Speech, Camperdown
- Assessments approx. 1 hour, telehealth or in person
- Clinicians work alongside the research group; also mentor other speech pathologists
- Email: asrc@uts.edu.au · Phone: +61 2 9514 5314
- Their stated position: "telehealth is just as effective as going to a clinic" and is "suitable for ALL ages"

**Why this one.** For a school age child specifically, this is where the Oakville program and the school-age protocols come from. Even if you end up with a local clinician for weekly sessions, a single assessment here is worth the money for the treatment plan alone.

- Clinic: https://www.stutteringtreatment.com.au/
- Research centre: https://www.uts.edu.au/research/centres/australian-stuttering-research-centre

### Queensland Health — Gold Coast Child Development Service
Public, free. Start here in parallel with private options, because the waitlist is the constraint.

- Phone: 07 5687 9183 (option 2)
- Email: cdsgoldcoast@health.qld.gov.au
- Referral pathway and eligibility need to be confirmed by phone — the public pages are thin on stuttering specifics.
- https://www.goldcoast.health.qld.gov.au/our-services/childrens-services/community-child-health/speak-with-me

### Speech Pathology Australia — Find a Speech Pathologist
The authoritative national directory. Filter by fluency/stuttering and by telehealth. Use this rather than SEO aggregator sites, which list clinics by advertising spend rather than expertise.

- https://www.speechpathologyaustralia.org.au/

Free initial phone consult with a speech pathologist. (07) 5679 5609 · roar@littlelanguage.com.au · https://www.littlelanguage.com.au/

- **Banter Speech & Language** — Sydney-based, telehealth Australia-wide, publishes clearly and honestly about the Lidcombe Program including its limitations. 02 8757 3838 · https://www.banterspeech.com.au/lidcombe-program/

## Global — specialist centres and telehealth

### Michael Palin Centre for Stammering, London
The leading school-age centre internationally. Home of Palin PCI and Palin STSC(8–14).

- Palin STSC(8–14): 10 weekly one-hour sessions plus reviews, both parents attending
- **Sessions can be delivered remotely** — worth an enquiry about international telehealth given the timezone works reasonably for Australian evenings/UK mornings
- https://michaelpalincentreforstammering.org/

### Stuttering Foundation of America
Not a clinic — the best free resource library in the world, plus a referral list of specialists. Their teacher materials are the ones cited in §4.

- https://www.stutteringhelp.org/

### STAMMA (British Stammering Association)
UK charity. Excellent plain-English resources and a helpline. Maintains information on the Michael Palin Centre.

- https://stamma.org/

## Peer support and community (Australia)

Therapy treats speech. Community treats isolation. Both matter — see the psychosocial evidence in §1.

- **STUTTA — Australian Stuttering Association.** Community by and for people who stutter. "Speak Freely" online meetups, 1st and 3rd Wednesday monthly, 7:00pm AEST. Welcomes family members. https://stutta.org/
- **Australian Speak Easy Association.** Long-standing peak body and support group network. https://speakeasy.org.au/
- **SAY: Australia** (Stuttering Association for the Young) — youth-focused.

## Paying for it — Australia, as at September 2026

### Medicare, expanded 1 March 2026 (the important one)

A dedicated pathway now exists for stuttering specifically. This replaced the need to go through a Chronic Condition Management Plan.

- **Eligible conditions:** stuttering, speech sound disorders (articulation, phonological, childhood apraxia, dysarthria), cleft lip/palate
- **Age:** under 25
- **Sessions:** up to **8 assessment** sessions and up to **20 treatment** sessions — a **lifetime** cap before age 25, not annual
- **Referral:** from a GP, consultant physician or specialist. **No care plan required.**
- **Rebate:** $87.25 per session (as at 16 Feb 2026)
- **MBS items:** assessment 82005, 93033, 93041 · treatment 82020, 93036, 93044

**Action item: book a GP appointment and ask for a referral under the new stuttering item numbers.** Quote the item numbers — this is new enough that not every GP knows about it.

### Chronic Condition Management Plan (the older pathway)
Up to 5 allied health sessions per calendar year, rebate around $61.80. Still available and can be used **alongside** the new pathway, just not for the same session on the same day. Lower rebate, annual rather than lifetime.

### NDIS
Possible where stuttering causes substantial functional impairment. Most stuttering alone will not meet the disability access criteria; it is more commonly funded where there is a co-occurring diagnosis. Worth exploring, not worth counting on.

### Private health insurance
Extras cover with speech pathology. Check annual limits and whether telehealth is claimable.

### Public
Queensland Health Child Development Service — free, waitlisted.

**Practical combination:** get on the Queensland Health waitlist today, get the GP referral for the new Medicare items this month, and book a one-off telehealth assessment with the ASRC clinic to get a proper plan. Then use the 20 rebated treatment sessions against whoever the plan says should deliver it.
